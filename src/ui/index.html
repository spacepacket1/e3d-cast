How to pay for Cast credits

Card (recommended) — Stripe Checkout packs on the workspace. No wallet required. After payment you return here with a credit key ready to render.

Ethereum E3D — native token on Ethereum mainnet. Send E3D to the treasury and register the transaction hash.

Base wE3D — wrapped E3D on Base. Faster / cheaper gas for agents and frequent top-ups.

On-chain rate: 1 E3D = 1,000 credits. Credits are spent per job at submission time.
