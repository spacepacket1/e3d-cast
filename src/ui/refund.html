Refund Policy

Last updated August 2026.

This is a basic interim policy while Cast is early — not a substitute for full legal review. Questions or a dispute: support@e3d.ai.

Failed renders

If a render job fails on our end after credits were spent, those credits are automatically refunded to your credit key — you don't need to ask. This happens the next time the job's status is checked, which the workspace does on its own right after submission.

Unused credit packs

Credits are a digital good, issued instantly on purchase. If you haven't used any credits from a pack yet, contact support@e3d.ai within 14 days of purchase and we'll review it case by case.

Successful renders

Credits spent on a job that completed successfully are not refundable — you received the output you paid for, even if the result doesn't match what you had in mind stylistically.

Card disputes

Please reach out to support@e3d.ai before filing a chargeback with your card issuer — most issues can be resolved faster directly.
