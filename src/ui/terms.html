Terms of Service

Last updated August 2026.

This is a basic interim policy covering the essentials while Cast is early — not a substitute for full legal review. If anything here is unclear, contact support@e3d.ai before purchasing.

The service

Cast (cast.e3d.ai) turns podcast audio or a transcript into a finished video. Rendering uses third-party services (transcription, narration, and stock footage providers) — Cast is a wrapper around a real pipeline, and render time and output vary with input length and complexity.

Payment and credits

Card purchases are processed by Stripe. Credits are issued to a single-use key tied to your purchase; Cast does not have a login system, so that key is your only proof of purchase — save it. Credits are consumed when a render job is submitted, not when it's queued or quoted. If a job fails on our end before completing, spent credits are automatically refunded to your key.

Acceptable use

Don't submit content you don't have the rights to use, or content intended to harass, defraud, or violate the law. Cast may decline or remove jobs that violate this.

No guarantees

Cast is provided as-is. Render quality depends on your input and on third-party services outside our direct control. We don't guarantee a specific output or turnaround time.
